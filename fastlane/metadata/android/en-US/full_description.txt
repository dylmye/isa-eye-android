Managing multiple Individual Savings Accounts while keeping on top of your annual allowance can be complicated. ISA Eye helps you keep an eye on Indiviual Saving Account balances across your bank accounts, investment managers and more. It's easy to make sure you don't go over your allowances - just add your accounts and keep your contributions up to date.

With ISA Eye, you can track your main and Junior ISA allowances, set savings goals that work for you, learn tips and tricks on maximising your allowance to make the most of your tax-free benefits, and more.

ISA Eye supports:

⋆ Saving account balances from 2017/2018 onwards
⋆ Flexible/non-flexible accounts
⋆ Types of ISAs with separate allowances, like the Lifetime ISA

Your data is completely safe in ISA Eye: it never leaves your device so nobody but you can see your accounts and balances. The app never has access to any of your accounts.
