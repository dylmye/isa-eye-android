# What is an ISA?

Really nothing crazy: Individual Savings Accounts are an efficient way to save and invest your money.

They were created by the Government in the late 90s to encourage people to invest in their future instead of spending it all on payday.

The main benefit of all ISAs is that you don't have to pay tax on the profit you make from the money in the account, from interest and capital gains (like growth in stock value).

There are some restrictions on what you can do with ISAs and just how much you can save in them, but other than that there's nothing special about them!
