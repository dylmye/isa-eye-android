# Maximising your ISA allowance

> The tips in this article are not intended as financial advice and ISAs are not suitable for everyone. Consult with a financial adviser before making important decisions about your money.

## Be aware of all of the allowances

You should be aware of the "global" allowance that most of your ISAs share, but also know your sub-allowances for Lifetime and Junior ISAs! You can find a breakdown of all of your allowances in the account tab of the ISA Eye app, by pressing the notes icon in the tax year selector. It's also super easy to keep an eye (pun intended) on how much of your allowance remains this year from the top of the accounts tab.

## Understand flexibility and make the most of it

Having a flexible ISA means the freedom to withdraw and contribute whenever suits you. The confidence of controlling what is and isn't in your ISA is underrated. Not all providers offer flexible ISAs so make sure to check before applying - if it's not mentioned on the provider's website, reach out to their customer support.

ISA Eye helps you keep track of your flexible ISAs and warns you if you're withdrawing from a non-flexible ISA.

## Don't DIY a transfer

There's a proper process to transfer all of your historic contributions from one provider to another. Withdrawing means you lose the tax-exempt status of that money: once it's out of the ISA it can't go back in without using more of the current tax year's allowance. You can initiate the transfer with the receiving provider.

## Shop around regularly

Especially around the end of the tax year, you'll find inflation-busting options for Cash ISAs and transfer/sign-up deals for all types of ISAs. It's worth moving if you make more tax-free gains from the same amount of contributions as it doesn't affect your allowance!

Just make sure there's no catches: not every provider supports transfers in/out, and some rates are contingent on making no withdrawals or contributing a minimum amount.

## Just because you can withdraw, doesn't mean you should

The power of compound growth - that is, not withdrawing interest and profit earned - is just as undervalued as a flexible ISA!

Some types of ISAs carry heavy penalties for withdrawing: unauthorised Lifetime ISA withdrawals charge a 25% fee for withdrawals that ends up leaving you with [less than you put in](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2025/09/lifetime-isa-treasury-responds/). Some Cash ISAs with headline interest rates have penalties that reduce the interest rate after a small number of withdrawals.

## Use it or lose it

The tax year runs from the 6th of April to the 5th of April, not calendar years. Your left-over allowance is not rolled over to the next year. While of course you should only save what you can afford and prioritise paying down debt over savings, keep an eye on how soon the 5th of April is to contribute as efficiently as possible! The ISA Eye app features a countdown to make this easier.
