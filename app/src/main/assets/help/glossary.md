# Glossary

Some common terms used when talking about ISAs.

- **ISA** (or **Account**): Individual Savings Account, a tax-exempt type of savings or investment account.
- **Allowance**: The maximum amount of money you can contribute to your ISA accounts in a given tax year.
- **Subscription**: Money paid into an ISA that lowers the allowance for the tax year it's paid in for.
- **Tax year**: Whereas a calendar year is the 1st Jan - 31st Dec, a tax year is when your allowance starts and finishes, and is always the 6th Apr to the 5th Apr.
- **Provider** (or **ISA manager**): The business (usually a bank, investment firm, or platform) that manages your ISA account and is legally responsible for it.
- **Flexible**: Whether the allowance the ISA uses can be returned when money is taken out of the ISA. The flexible status is decided by the provider.
- **Transfer**: The process of moving an ISA's funds from one provider to another.
- **In-specie**: A transfer where the investments within the ISA are retained, not sold, when being passed to the new provider.
